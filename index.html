Common questions

What is the GLP-1 Weight Loss Program? −
A 3-month, medically-guided weight-loss program combining GLP-1 therapy oversight, home metabolic testing, doctor consultations, a personalised dietitian plan, and live fitness coaching.
Is the GLP-1 medication included in the price? +
No. The medication cost is separate and not part of this package. Your MD doctor will guide you on the medication during your consultation.
How does the program work? +
After enrolling, you’ll have a metabolic test at home, consult an MD doctor for medical guidance, get a personalised diet plan from a clinical dietitian, and join live coach-led fitness sessions — all over 3 months.
Who is this program for? +
Adults 18+ looking for medically-supervised weight loss. Whether GLP-1 therapy is right for you is confirmed by the MD doctor during your consultation.
Is the program online or in-person? +
The program is administered online. The metabolic health checkup is at-home.
